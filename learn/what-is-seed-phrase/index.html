What are 12 words?

When you make a Blitz wallet, you get 12 ordinary words. Those words help restore your wallet. Here is what the words mean and how to protect them.

Get Blitz and save yours What self-custody means
Plain English

What is a seed phrase?

A seed phrase is a set of words that creates your wallet. Blitz uses 12 words. In a compatible app, they can restore the corresponding wallet on another device.

It is your key, not your password

A password can be reset by a company. These words cannot. They create your keys right on your phone. Blitz never sees them.

Your money is not on your phone

Your Bitcoin lives in the network. Your phone just holds the key. Lose the phone and the money still sits there, waiting for your words.

Same words, same wallet

Type the same 12 words in order on any phone and the same wallet opens. That is how recovery works. It is also why sharing them gives everything away.

Never share your 12 words

Blitz support will not ask you to send them by email, chat, or form. Official wallet restore and recovery tools may ask you to enter them. Verify the destination before entering a phrase.

Do this once

Back up your words in 5 steps

Do it the day you create your wallet. It takes five minutes.

Write them on paper

Copy all 12 words by hand in exact order. Number them 1 to 12. Use pen and paper. No screenshots. No typing into notes apps.

Check every word twice

Read the paper back against the screen. A wrong word can prevent the wallet keys from being restored. Fix mistakes now while the screen is still open.

Store the paper offline

Keep it somewhere dry and protected, such as a locked drawer or safe. Avoid photos, email, and cloud drives because digital copies can be exposed if an account or device is compromised.

Keep a protected backup

If you lose the phrase and can no longer access the wallet on a device, you may be unable to restore the wallet keys. Blitz cannot recreate the phrase. Recovery of Spark funds also depends on the supported recovery path and operator availability.

Right and wrong

FAQ

Questions beginners ask

What is a seed phrase in simple terms?

A seed phrase is a set of 12 words that creates a wallet. Anyone who obtains the phrase controls the wallet. Keep it private and enter it only in a verified restore or recovery flow.

What if I lose my phone?

On a new device, your 12 words can restore your wallet.

What if I lose the 12 words?

If you lose the phrase and can no longer access the wallet on a device, you will be unable to restore the wallet. Blitz cannot recreate your phrase.

Can I screenshot them?

Digital screenshots may sync to other devices or online backups and can be exposed if an account is compromised. Prefer a written offline backup, or another secure method you understand.

Will Blitz ever ask for my words?

Blitz support will never ask you to send the words by email, chat, or form. The official Blitz app and browser-based recovery tool may ask you to enter them. Verify the official source and understand the risks before using a recovery tool. Read the recovery guidance.

How is this different from a bank password?

A bank holds your money and can reset your login. With self-custody you hold the money, so the words replace both the password and the bank.

Make your backup today

Write down your 12 words and keep them private and offline. They’re how you restore your wallet if you lose your phone.
