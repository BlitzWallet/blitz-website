What are 12 words?

When you make a Blitz wallet, you get 12 ordinary words. Those words are your money's only backup. Here is what they mean in plain English.

Plain English

What is a seed phrase?

A seed phrase is a master key written as words. Blitz gives you 12 of them. Together they can rebuild your wallet on any phone.

It is your key, not your password

A password can be reset by a company. These words cannot. They create your keys right on your phone. Blitz never sees them.

Your money is not on your phone

Your Bitcoin lives in the network. Your phone just holds the key. Lose the phone and the money still sits there, waiting for your words.

Same words, same wallet

Type the same 12 words in order on any phone and the same wallet opens. That is how recovery works. It is also why sharing them gives everything away.

Never share your 12 words

Blitz support will never ask for them. No giveaway, helper, or website needs them. Anyone who asks is trying to steal your money.

Do this once

Back up your words in 4 steps

Do it the day you create your wallet. It takes five minutes.

Write them on paper

Copy all 12 words by hand in exact order. Number them 1 to 12. Use pen and paper. No screenshots. No typing into notes apps.

Check every word twice

Read the paper back against the screen. One wrong word can lock you out. Fix mistakes now while the screen is still open.

Store the paper offline

Keep it somewhere dry and private that only you can reach. A locked drawer or safe works. Never in photos, email, or cloud drives.

Lose your phone? Restore with the words

Install Blitz on the new phone. Choose Restore. Type the 12 words in order. Your balance and history come back. You can also recover with the open-source recovery tool if Blitz is ever gone.

Lose the words? You lose the money

Say it plainly. No words means no recovery. No support ticket fixes it. This is the price of owning your money outright, and the paper backup is how you pay it safely.

FAQ

Questions beginners ask

What is a seed phrase in simple terms?

Twelve ordinary words that act as the master key to your Bitcoin. Whoever has them in order can open your wallet.

What if I lose my phone?

Install Blitz on a new phone, choose Restore, type the 12 words. Your balance returns.

What if I lose the 12 words?

You lose access. No one can reset them, not even Blitz. Guard them like cash.

Can I screenshot them?

No. Screenshots sync to cloud backups where hackers look. Paper and pen only.

Will Blitz ever ask for my words?

Never. Any message asking for them is a scam. Learn what self-custody means so the rule makes sense.

How is this different from a bank password?

A bank holds your money and can reset your login. With self-custody you hold the money, so the words replace both the password and the bank.
