FAQ

Common questions

Can I send Bitcoin to a friend with no wallet?

Yes. Send a gift link. They tap it, make a free Blitz wallet, and claim the money. How gifts work.

Do I need my friend's Bitcoin address?

No. Save them as a contact and pay by name. For other wallets, use their Lightning Address or QR code instead.

What does it cost?

Blitz to Blitz contact payments carry no Blitz fee. Lightning cost a tiny network fee. On-chain costs a miner fee that moves with traffic. You see it before you confirm.

How fast is it?

Contact, Lightning, and Spark payments land in seconds. On-chain takes 30+ minutes to confirm.

What if I pay the wrong person?

Bitcoin payments cannot be undone. Check the name and amount first. Send a small test for new contacts.
