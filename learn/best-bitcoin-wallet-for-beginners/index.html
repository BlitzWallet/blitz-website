Best Bitcoin wallet for beginners

New to Bitcoin? You want three things. Your own keys. Low fees. And an app that feels familiar. Here is how the popular options compare.

What matters

Five questions to ask before you pick

These questions help you compare the tradeoffs behind the marketing.

Who holds the keys?

If you hold the seed phrase, you control the wallet keys, but a payment route may still depend on a service or operator.

What do you pay?

Compare the fees for the payment route you plan to use. Fixed and percentage fees can affect small payments differently.

Do you need an ID?

Blitz does not require government ID to create a wallet. Other providers may request identity information for specific Bitcoin services, depending on the service and region. Check their current requirements.

Does it work worldwide?

Bitcoin is an open network, but wallet services and payment providers may set regional limits. Check the current terms for each provider where you live.

Can you pay friends easily?

A saved contact can help avoid copying a long address. Check the recipient and amount before confirming any payment.

What if you lose your phone?

Ask what a backup restores. Some wallet designs need more than a seed phrase to move funds through every payment route.

Our take

Why beginners pick Blitz

Honest summary. Each option fits someone. Here is who fits where.

Pick Blitz for contacts and multiple payment routes

Blitz does not store your 12-word seed phrase. Use contacts, Lightning, on-chain Bitcoin, Stablecoins, and supported offline Spark receive flows. Read the security and recovery guide.

Pick Coinbase to buy first

Coinbase offers buying services through an exchange account. Exchange custody and wallet products can have different key models. Review its current custody, fees, and withdrawal terms before deciding where to hold Bitcoin.

Pick Cash App for dollars plus a little Bitcoin

Cash App combines dollar services and Bitcoin features for eligible users. Check current availability, identity requirements, fees, and custody terms in your region before using it.

Blitz and Muun use different payment models

Blitz uses Spark for supported wallet payments and also supports Lightning and on-chain Bitcoin. Its users control a 12-word seed phrase. Muun has a different payment and recovery model; compare each wallet's current documentation and displayed fees. Read the Blitz recovery guide.

Already own Bitcoin somewhere else?

Select Receive in Blitz and choose the same payment type or network that the sending wallet supports. Check the address, amount, route, and fee, then consider a small test payment. Full steps are in how to receive Bitcoin.

The one rule for every wallet

A seed phrase derives wallet keys, but the wallet's architecture determines what those keys authorize and how funds can be recovered. Keep a protected backup and compare each provider's recovery model. Learn what 12 words mean and what self-custody means.

Beginner questions

Is Blitz Wallet really free for beginners?

The app has no monthly charge. Blitz-to-Blitz contact payments have no Blitz transaction fee. Network or swap fees may apply on other routes; review the displayed fee before confirming.

Do I need an ID to start a Bitcoin wallet?

Blitz does not require government ID to create a wallet. Other providers may request identity information for specific Bitcoin services, depending on the service and region. Check their current requirements.

Should I keep Bitcoin on Coinbase?

A Coinbase exchange account and Blitz use different custody models. Blitz users control a 12-word seed phrase. Compare each service's current recovery information.

Is Cash App or Blitz better for friends?
