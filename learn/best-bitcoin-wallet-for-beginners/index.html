What matters

Five questions to ask before you pick

Every wallet claims to be simple. These five questions cut through the marketing.

Who holds the keys?

If a company holds your keys, they control your money. If you hold them, only you do. Beginners should know which one they are getting.

What do you pay?

Look at receive fees and send fees. Small percentages add up fast on small amounts.

Do you need an ID?

Exchanges and payment apps require identity checks. True wallets do not. No ID means you can start right now.

Does it work worldwide?

Some apps only work in one country. Bitcoin is global. Your wallet should be too.

Can you pay friends easily?

Copying long addresses is where beginners make mistakes. Paying by contact name is safer and faster.

What if you lose your phone?

A good wallet gives you a backup that restores everything. Ask what the backup looks like before you add money.

FAQ

Beginner questions

Is Blitz Wallet really free for beginners?

Yes. The app is free. Some payments carry small network or swap fees shown before you confirm.

Do I need an ID to start a Bitcoin wallet?

Not with Blitz or Muun. Download and save your backup. Coinbase and Cash App need identity checks first.

Should I keep Bitcoin on Coinbase?

Coinbase is handy for buying. But they hold your keys. Move larger amounts to a wallet you control, like Blitz.

Is Cash App or Blitz better for friends?

Blitz if you want your own keys and worldwide reach. Cash App if you already use it daily and only want small US Bitcoin buys.

What is the difference between Blitz and Muun?

Both are self-custodial. Muun routes Lightning through on-chain swaps, so fees can spike. Blitz uses Spark and Lightning for tiny fees, plus contacts, gifts, pools, and dollars.

Can I move Bitcoin I already own into Blitz?

Yes. Tap Receive in Blitz, copy your address, and send a small test first. Then move the rest. See how to receive Bitcoin.
