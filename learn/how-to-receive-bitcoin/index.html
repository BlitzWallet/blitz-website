Step-by-step for beginners

How to receive Bitcoin in 6 easy steps

No technical knowledge needed — just follow what to tap.

Create your Blitz wallet

Download Blitz for iOS, Android, or Web. Write your 12-word recovery phrase on paper and store it safely. It is your only backup, and Blitz never sees it.

Share your Lightning Address

Share your Lightning address anywhere to receive instant payments from compatible Lightning wallets.

Request an exact amount with a QR

Create an invoice with an amount and an optional note, then show the QR code. The sender scans it to pay instantly.

Use a regular Bitcoin address

For exchange withdrawals or large transfers, use an on-chain address. The address beginning with bc1.... Payments usually confirm in 30+ minutes, and the network fee varies with demand.

Get paid in USDT / USDC

Create an accumulation address for USDT or USDC, and share it with the correct network. Blitz supports Arbitrum, Base, BNB Chain, Ethereum, Optimism, Plasma, Polygon, Solana, and Tron.

Keep as dollars or Bitcoin

Move between BTC and USDB in seconds whenever your spending or saving needs change.

Beginner examples

Which option fits you? Real examples

All of these put money in the same Blitz wallet. You just pick the simplest one for your sender.

Lightning Address

Add username@blitzwalletapp.com to your bio, invoices, or tip page so anyone with a Lightning wallet can choose an amount and pay instantly. The address uses your Blitz username and also powers blitzwalletapp.com/u/yourname.

Spark or Lightning QR code

For an exact in-person request, choose create invoice, enter the amount and a note, then show the QR code. Shops can use Blitz Point of Sale to add a logo and display prices in local currency.

Regular Bitcoin address (on-chain)

Use this for an exchange withdrawal or a larger transfer. Copy your address beginning with bc1..., and follow its status in Blitz while the network confirms it.

Digital dollars: USDT and USDC

When the sender has USDT or USDC, create an accumulation address for their network and choose whether to receive BTC or USDB. Blitz supports Arbitrum, Base, BNB Chain, Ethereum, Optimism, Plasma, Polygon, Solana, and Tron.

Swap between dollars and Bitcoin

Use our swap feature when you receive dollars but want to save in Bitcoin, or receive Bitcoin but need stable spending money. Choose a direction and review the displayed rate and fee before confirming.

Blitz contact

When both people use Blitz, add the recipient as a contact, choose their name, and send or requset without an address or QR code. Contact payments, and they appear alongside every other payment in the same wallet.

FAQ

Common questions

My sender only has USDT on Tron. Can they still pay me?

Yes. In Blitz create a Tron digital dollar address (accumulation address) and choose to auto-convert to Bitcoin or keep as dollars. Give that address to the sender. They send USDT on Tron, and it shows up in Blitz as the Bitcoin or dollars you chose. The same works for USDT or USDC on 8 other networks: Arbitrum, Base, BNB Chain, Ethereum, Optimism, Plasma, Polygon, and Solana. Learn about accumulation addresses.

What is the difference between Lightning, Spark, and on-chain?

Lightning and Spark are for fast, cheap payments. Think of them like sending a text. They are great for everyday amounts and arrive instantly. On-chain is a regular Bitcoin transfer on the main Bitcoin network. Think of it like a bank wire. It takes 10 to 60 minutes to confirm but is good for large amounts or moving money from an exchange.

Should I receive in Bitcoin (BTC) or dollars (USDB)?

Receive it how you want to keep it. If you are worried about short term changes in price, keep it as dollars (USDB). If you want long-term savings, keep it as Bitcoin. You can also swap between dollars and Bitcoin in seconds inside Blitz at any time. Many people keep everyday spending in USDB and savings in BTC. What is USDB? USDB is digital dollars on Bitcoin, issued by Brale and backed 1:1 by US Treasuries. Like all stablecoins, it has issuer risk.

Is this safe? Who controls my money?

You do. Blitz is self-custodial, which means only your 12-word recovery phrase can move your money. Blitz never has access to your funds. Keep those 12 words written on paper and never share them. If you lose your phone, you can use them to recover your wallet by downloading blitz again.
