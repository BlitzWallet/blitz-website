FAQ

Common questions

Is USDB safe to hold?

USDB is designed to be backed by Treasuries and cash held by Brale. Check the latest reserve reports, audits, and redemption terms before you decide if it fits your needs. Read the USDB details.

How long will rewards be available?

Keep at least 10 USDB to get up to 6 percent back, paid each day in BTC. Terms can change, so check the app for the current offer. Like all stablecoins, USDB carries issuer and platform risk.

What are the fees?

You pay a small fee when you swap, which you see before you confirm. There is no fee just to hold USDB.
