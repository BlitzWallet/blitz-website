What self-custody means

A self-custody Bitcoin wallet means you hold the keys. Not a bank, not an exchange, not Blitz. Your 12-word seed phrase is the backup that lets you recover the wallet.

The basics

What self-custody means

Think of a safe you own. Only your key opens it. Blitz helps you use it, but cannot open it for you.

You hold the seed phrase

Blitz does not store your 12-word backup. Anyone who gets the words may be able to control the wallet.

Operators help process transfers

Spark operators participate in ordinary Spark state changes. If they are unavailable or do not cooperate, normal Spark transfers can be delayed or stop.

Recovery has requirements

If Spark operators go offline, Spark’s unilateral exit lets you withdraw to Bitcoin without them, though today it’s a technical process that isn’t built into Blitz. Our security page explains the steps and limits.

Never share your seed phrase

Never send your seed phrase by email, chat, or form. Blitz also publishes a browser-based recovery tool that asks for a seed; using it means entering the words on a website. Verify the official project and understand its limits before deciding whether to use it. Anyone who gets your words may control the wallet.

How to do it right

How to back up your wallet

Write it on paper

Copy the 12 words in order by hand and double check them. Keep the backup in a secure place.

Never share or screenshot

Anyone with the words can take the funds. Do not email, save to the cloud, or share them. Blitz will never ask for them.

Test your recovery

After backing up, try restoring on another device, then wipe it. If it works once, it will work when you need it.

Add a PIN & biometrics

Blitz lets you lock the app with PIN, Face ID or fingerprint. Your wallet secret stays encrypted on device.

FAQ

Common questions

Can Blitz help if I lose my backup?

Blitz cannot retrieve your seed phrase. If you lose it and have no other copy, you will lose access to the wallet. Keep the words private and backed up.

Is self-custody harder to use?

Blitz is designed to make day-to-day payments simple, but you are responsible for keeping your seed phrase safe.

What about large amounts?

Blitz is designed for day-to-day use. Before keeping a large balance in any wallet, understand its payment and recovery model and choose a setup that fits your needs.
