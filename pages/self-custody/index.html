What self-custody means

A self-custody Bitcoin wallet means you hold the keys. Not a bank, not an exchange, not Blitz. Your 12-word seed phrase is the backup that lets you recover the wallet.

The basics

What self-custody means

Think of a safe you own. Only your key opens it. Blitz helps you use it, but cannot open it for you.

You hold the keys

Your wallet is made from 12 random words on your device. Whoever has those words controls the funds. Blitz and Spark cannot move them.

No freeze, no reverse

With custodial apps, a company can freeze or reverse a payment. With self-custody, only you can send. Like cash in your pocket.

Recover anywhere

Enter the 12-word backup in Blitz or the open-source spark-recover tool. Your funds reappear. If you lose the backup, you lose the funds.

Never share your seed phrase

Only enter your 12 words when you are intentionally restoring your wallet inside the official Blitz app or a trusted, compatile wallet. Never type them into a website, form, chat, or send them to anyone. Anyone who gets your seed phrase can take your money.

How to do it right

How to back up your wallet

Write it on paper

Copy the 12 words in order by hand and double check them. Keep the backup in a secure place.

Never share or screenshot

Anyone with the words can take the funds. Do not email, save to the cloud, or share them. Blitz will never ask for them.

Test your recovery

After backing up, try restoring on another device, then wipe it. If it works once, it will work when you need it.

Add a PIN & biometrics

Blitz lets you lock the app with PIN, Face ID or fingerprint. Your wallet secret stays encrypted on device.

FAQ

Common questions

Can Blitz help if I lose my backup?

No. That is the point of self-custody. If Blitz could recover without the backup, it could also move your money without you. Keep the 12 words safe.

Is self-custody harder to use?

No. Save your 12 words once. After that, Blitz works like any other payments app. You can pay contacts, send gifts, and use pools the same way.

What about large amounts?

Blitz is considered a "hot wallet". This means while your keys are yours, it's meant to be a daily spending wallet insted of a savings wallet.
