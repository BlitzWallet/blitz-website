How it works

Create gifts in seconds, claim with Blitz

Make one or many gifts at once. The person who gets the link needs a Blitz wallet to claim it. If they are new, they can create one from the link. If the gift expires, you can reclaim it in the app.

Choose the gift

Choose the amount and USD or BTC denomination, set the quantity and duration, and add a short description.

Share the link

Blitz creates a claim link such as blitzwalletapp.com/gift/abc…. Send it by WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

They claim, or you reclaim

The recipient opens the link in Blitz and claims the gift. If it expires first, tap Reclaim to return the funds to your wallet.

Before you send

Set the rules while you create the link

A gift is a specific amount with a specific expiry. Confirm the denomination and duration before you share it, and remember that the recipient must claim in Blitz.

BTC or USD denomination

Choose BTC when you want the recipient to receive Bitcoin, or USD when you want the gift to use USDB. The selected denomination is part of the gift setup.

Expiry is not automatic reclaim

When the gift expires, it becomes eligible for reclaim. Open Blitz and tap Reclaim to return the funds to your wallet.

FAQ

Quick answers

Who can claim a gift?

Anyone with a Blitz wallet can claim it. A new recipient can open the link, create a wallet in Blitz, and then tap Claim.

What if it expires?

After expiry, tap Reclaim in Blitz to return the funds to your wallet. Reclaiming is an action you take; the funds do not return automatically.

Can I send dollars?

Yes. Choose USD as the denomination when you create the gift. It sends as USDB. Learn about USDB or Dollar Goals.

Are gifts free?

No extra Blitz gift fee. Standard send fees apply (tiny for Spark/Lightning, variable for on-chain).

Are there gift limits or denominations?

The app shows the available amount, denomination, quantity, and expiry options when you create a gift. Review those settings before confirming because the gift link represents the selected BTC or USD denomination.
