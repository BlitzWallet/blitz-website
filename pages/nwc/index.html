FAQ

Wallet Connect questions

What is Wallet Connect?

Wallet Connect (Nostr Wallet Connect, NWC) is an open protocol that lets apps ask your wallet to create invoices, check your balance or send payments. In Blitz, connected apps use a separate Wallet Connect account, and you choose what each app may do.

Can a connected app reach my main Blitz balance?

No. Connected apps only use your Wallet Connect account, which has its own balance. Your main balance is never available to them.

How do I disconnect an app?

Open Blitz, go to Settings → Wallet Connect, choose the connection and delete it. The app loses access immediately.

Why does Wallet Connect need notifications?

Your wallet lives on your phone. Requests from apps reach Blitz as push notifications, so Blitz can answer them even when the app is closed.
