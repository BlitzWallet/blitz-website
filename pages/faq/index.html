The basics

Start here

What is Blitz Wallet?

Blitz is a self-custodial wallet for everyday payments on mobile and web. Pay contacts, send gifts, collect money with Pools, create payment links, swap to USDB, or accept payments as a merchant.

Is Blitz self-custodial?

Yes. Your 12-word backup generates your keys on your device. Neither Blitz nor Spark can move your funds. Back up the words on paper. See how self-custody works.

Is it free?

The app is free. However some supported payment types have a small fee associated with thenm.

Which devices work?

iOS (App Store), Android (Play Store + Zapstore), and any browser via wallet.blitzwalletapp.com. Same wallet logic everywhere. Download options.

Payments

How paying works

What is Spark & Lightning?

Lightning is Bitcoin’s instant Layer 2. Spark is a newer Bitcoin Layer 2 by Lightspark using statechains + FROST signatures that makes Lightning easy: no channel management, offline receive, under-a-second settlement.

Can I pay any Lightning QR?

Yes. One scanner handles BOLT11, LNURL, Lightning addresses, on-chain invoices, and Spark invoices. You can also receive at you@blitzwalletapp.com.

What are Gifts, Pools and Paylinks?

Gifts = send via a shareable link that the recipient claims in Blitz. Pools = collect as a group with one shared link. Paylinks = payment links that anyone can pay from any compatible Lightning wallet or with USDT/USDC. The product adds no extra gift or Pool fee, but the underlying payment or network fee still applies.

Do I need the recipient’s invoice?

Not for Blitz contacts. Pick a name and send. For other people, use their Lightning address or scan their QR code or invoice.

Can I get paid while offline?

Yes nothing stops you from receiving your money.

Money & stablecoins

Bitcoin & dollars, same wallet

What is USDB?

USDB is a dollar-pegged stablecoin on Spark issued by Brale, backed 1:1 by Treasury bills and cash equivalents. Hold 10 or more USDB to earn up to 6% back, paid daily in BTC. Program terms can change, so check the app. Stablecoins carry issuer and platform risk. USDB details.

Can I use USDT & USDC?

Yes. Receive USDC or USDT via accumulation addresses on 9 supported networks: Arbitrum, Base, BNB Chain, Ethereum, Optimism, Plasma, Polygon, Solana, and Tron. Blitz can auto-convert to BTC or USDB. Swap inside Blitz via Flashnet.

Why dollars on Bitcoin?

Some people have a hard time with Bitcoin's short-term volatility. Dollars can make everyday spending more stable. Hold USDB for spending and Bitcoin for savings, then swap between them in seconds.

Is there a fee to hold dollars?

No holding fee. Small swap fees apply. Hold 10 or more USDB to earn up to 6% back, paid daily in BTC. Program terms can change, so check the app. Stablecoins carry issuer and platform risk.

Security & recovery

Keep control

What if I lose my phone?

Restore with your 12 words in Blitz or the open-source recovery tool. Funds return. Lose the words = lose the funds.

Is Blitz open-source?

Yes. Apache 2.0 at GitHub.

Do merchants pay more?

No monthly fee for Point of Sale. Same low fees as personal payments. POS details.
