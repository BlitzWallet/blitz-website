FAQ

Quick answers

Can people pay without Blitz?

Yes. The pool page accepts Lightning invoices plus USDT/USDC via integrated flows. No Blitz account needed to contribute.

Who can close a payment pool?

The pool creator decides when the collection is done, closes the pool, and moves the funds to their wallet. Contributors cannot close it through the pool.

Can contributors get a refund through the pool?

The pool does not provide a contributor refund or close flow. Contributors should agree on the purpose and refund arrangements with the pool creator before paying.

What does it cost?

No extra Blitz pool fee. Each contribution pays its normal tiny Lightning or swap fee.
