Someone set up a wallet just for you.

It's a Bitcoin wallet you share with someone you trust. Your own money to spend, with them looking out for you.

So what's a shared wallet?

You get your own balance to spend, send, and receive. The person who invited you can top it up, keep an eye out for you, and bring the wallet back if your phone ever goes missing.

App Store Google Play

Free, and no account or email needed.

Getting started
01
Download Blitz Wallet

Grab the free app from the App Store or Google Play.

02
Tap “Join managed wallet”

You'll spot it on the “Set up your wallet” screen.

03
Scan their code

Ask the person who invited you to show their QR code, or just type in their username.

04
Confirm and you're in

They tap approve on their phone, and your wallet is ready. Welcome.
