FAQ

Accumulation address questions

What is an accumulation address?

It is a reusable address for receiving USDT or USDC on a supported network. Blitz routes the payment and converts it to BTC or USDB based on what you picked.

Which networks are supported?

Blitz supports Arbitrum, Base, BNB Chain, Ethereum, Optimism, Plasma, Polygon, Solana, and Tron. The sender must use the same network and token shown for your address.

What happens if a sender uses the wrong network?

Do not guess. A token sent on the wrong network may not arrive or may not be recoverable. Confirm the token, network, and address with the sender before they pay.
