Using Spark in Blitz

Everyday Bitcoin payments

Spark is a Bitcoin Layer 2 network. Blitz uses it to connect familiar wallet features with payments, contacts, and Lightning.

Pay without channel setup

You do not need to open Lightning channels, manage inbound liquidity, or run a payment node to use Blitz.

Use more than Spark

Blitz also supports Lightning payments and on-chain Bitcoin. The app presents payment details and any applicable fee before you confirm.

Payments with people and businesses

Use contacts, gifts, pools, Nostr Wallet Connect, and point of sale alongside the wallet's Bitcoin payment features.

Know the tradeoffs

Fast payments still have a trust model

Blitz does not store your 12-word seed phrase. Spark operators participate in ordinary Spark state changes, so seed ownership and the ability to move funds during an operator outage are separate questions.

Operators and normal transfers

Operators participate in normal Spark transfers. If they are unavailable or refuse to cooperate, those transfers can be delayed or stop. Read the Spark documentation and Blitz's own security explanation.

Recovery and operator outages

If operators stop signing, Spark’s unilateral exit lets you withdraw to the Bitcoin blockchain without them. Today it’s a command-line process with on-chain fees and timelock waits. Compare the current recovery evidence.

Common questions

Spark wallet questions

What is Spark in Blitz Wallet?

Spark is the Bitcoin Layer 2 network Blitz uses for wallet payments. Blitz also supports Lightning and on-chain Bitcoin.

Do Spark operators take part in payments?

Yes. Spark operators participate in ordinary Spark state changes. If they are unavailable or do not cooperate, normal Spark transfers may be delayed or stop.

Can I move Spark funds if operators are unavailable?

Spark’s unilateral exit lets you withdraw to the Bitcoin blockchain without operators, but today it’s a technical command-line process with on-chain fees and waiting periods, and small balances aren’t worth exiting. Read the security page.
