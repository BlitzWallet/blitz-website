A simpler way to pay

What a Lightning wallet does

Lightning is a Bitcoin payment network designed for everyday transactions.

Pay a Lightning request

Scan a compatible QR code or open a payment request. Check the amount, recipient, and displayed fee before you approve a payment.

Receive with an address

Share your Lightning address to receive without generating a new invoice for every payment. See how Blitz addresses work.

Pay people you know

Send to a Blitz contact by name. Blitz-to-Blitz contact payments have no Blitz transaction fee; other payment routes may have network or service fees.
