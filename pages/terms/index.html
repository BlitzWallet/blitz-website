8.6 Hot Wallet and Device-Compromise Risk

Blitz Wallet is a "hot wallet": your recovery phrase (seed) is stored on your mobile device using the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via expo-secure-store).

App-level authentication (PIN or biometric) is optional, and once the App is open it does not require additional authentication to send funds or to display your recovery phrase. As a result, any person or software with access to your unlocked device may be able to view your recovery phrase and irreversibly transfer your funds, including through:

  • Theft, loss, or unauthorized physical access to your device
  • Malware, spyware, or remote compromise of your device
  • Inspection of device memory (the seed is held in the App's memory while running)
  • A device backup that contains your seed (see below)

Device backups: On Android, the seed is stored on-device only and is never included in any backup. On iOS, current versions of the App exclude the seed from iCloud backups; however, if your device backups were configured before this change, an existing iCloud backup may still contain your seed. If you may be affected, consider moving your funds to a newly generated wallet.

Blitz Wallet cannot detect, prevent, reverse, or recover from any such compromise. We strongly recommend enabling PIN or biometric protection, keeping your device secure and updated, and using a dedicated cold-storage or hardware wallet for large or long-term holdings.

12.2 Seed Transmission

Critical Technical Disclosure:

To initialize your wallet, the App must perform a one-time, in-memory transmission of your Seed from your device's secure storage into the WebView Component during each wallet session. This transmission is necessary to enable wallet functionality including:

  • Sending and receiving payments
  • Getting wallet balance

  • Other wallet actions

12.3 Security Safeguards

We implement multiple security measures to protect the Processing System, including:

Cryptographic Protections:

  • Hash verification of the WebView bundle before execution to ensure file integrity
  • Session-specific keypair generation with random nonces for mutual authentication

  • Replay and duplication protection through sequential identifiers and timestamps

Isolation Measures:

  • Restrictive Content Security Policy (CSP) to mitigate script injection and cross-site scripting
  • WebView execution in an isolated, incognito context
  • Debugging, file system access, caching, and third-party network requests disabled
