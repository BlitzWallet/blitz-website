Create separate wallets for different needs. Keep your spending organized or manage an account for someone you help.

Two types

Personal and managed

Accounts are created under your main wallet, so one 12-word seed restores them all. Read the recovery details.

Personal accounts for you

Create “Spending,” “Savings,” or “Business” accounts to keep your money organized. Give each one its own name, emoji, Lightning address, and USD or BTC receiving preference, then manage its balance and history separately.

Managed accounts for others

Create an account for someone you help. Pair the account to their phone so they can use it, while you add or withdraw funds and see activity.

Recovery and boundaries

One wallet, clear responsibility

Accounts help you stay organized, but you still need to keep your main backup safe.

Restore the main wallet

Restore the main wallet's 12-word seed phrase; your accounts come back with it. See the security page.

Know who is responsible

Personal accounts hold your own money. Managed accounts are for someone you help. You stay responsible for recovery and funding.

Use separate addresses

Give each account its own Lightning address. Payments and history stay separate, and you can set each one to receive in USD or BTC.

FAQ

Questions about Blitz Accounts

Do sub-wallets need separate backups?

They are created under your main wallet, which you restore with its 12-word seed phrase. Keep that backup safe, and see the security page for recovery details.

Does each account have its own Lightning address?

Sort of. Every personal sub-account can have its own Lightning address and choose to receive in USD or BTC. Managed accounts also get their own Lightning address, based on the username the account holder chooses.

Can a managed account holder recover the account alone?

No. Pairing lets the other person use the managed account on their phone while recovery remains linked to your main wallet.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Install Blitz on a new device and restore the main wallet's 12-word seed phrase. Your accounts come back with it; see the security page for recovery details.
