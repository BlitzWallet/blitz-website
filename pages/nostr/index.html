FAQ

Nostr wallet questions

What is a NIP-05 address?

A NIP-05 address is a readable identifier such as you@blitzwalletapp.com that helps Nostr clients verify your identity.

What is Nostr Wallet Connect?

Nostr Wallet Connect lets a app request wallet actions through a named connection. In Blitz, you choose permissions and a spending budget for each connection.

Can a Nostr app spend all my funds?

A connected app can only use the permissions and budget you grant it. It can also only spend what is in your NWC sub-account never your main wallet. Review the connection name and limits, and revoke the connection when you no longer need it.

What happens if I delete a Nostr connection?

The service that uses that connection string will no longer work and you will have to link a new connection string.
