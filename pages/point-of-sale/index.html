FAQ

POS questions

Do I need new hardware?

No. Use your phone or desktop at pay.blitzwalletapp.com. Customers scan a QR and pay from any compatible Lightning wallet or with USDT/USDC.

How do payouts work?

Payments settle to your Blitz wallet. Configure items, tips, and view sales history in the POS flow.

Do customers need Blitz to pay?

No. Customers can pay the QR code from any compatible Lightning wallet, or use supported USDT or USDC flows.
