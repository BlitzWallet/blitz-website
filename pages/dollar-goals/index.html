FAQ

Dollar Goal questions

What are Dollar Goals?

Dollar Goals use USDB, dollars on Bitcoin via Spark, to help you set a general or specific savings target. You can add Bitcoin or dollars, withdraw either, and track activity.

Can I add or withdraw Bitcoin?

Yes. Add Bitcoin and convert it to USDB, or add dollars directly. You can withdraw to BTC or dollars, and Blitz shows the conversion rate and any swap fee before you confirm.

What rewards does a Dollar Goal offer?

Hold 10 or more USDB to earn up to 6% back, paid daily in BTC. Program terms can change, so check the app. Stablecoins carry issuer and platform risk.

Are Dollar Goals investment advice?

No. Dollar Goals are a wallet feature for organizing funds. Rewards, stablecoins, and Bitcoin each carry risks; review current terms and decide what is appropriate for you.
